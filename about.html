Club Introduction

We are the UTAR E-Sports Club, established in 2020 at Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR). We are dedicated to promoting competitive gaming and building a vibrant e-sports community on campus.

Our club currently has50+ active members across multiple game titles including PUBG,CS GO,DOTA2,LOL,ROCKET LEAGUE, and VALORANT.

We organize regular intra-university tournaments, participate in external collegiate leagues, and provide training sessions, live streaming, and community events. Whether you're a casual gamer or a competitive player, everyone is welcome!
